
# What is CRM ? 

- CRM stands for Customer Relationship Management.
- It is used to manage company relationships and used to interact with customers.
- It helps in improving businness growth and profit.


# Why companies use Salesforce

- Improves sales and employee productivity
- It lowers production cost
- Focuses on  Automation which helps in handling repetitive tasks so that important work might not miss.
- Manage customer information

 
##  What is Account
Represents a company or organization.

##  What is Contact
- Represents a individual person.
- It includes the names, emails, and phone numbers of the people.

## What is Opportunity
 It Represents a potential sales deal.
